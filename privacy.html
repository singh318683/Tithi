Tithi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Ikshana Solutions LLC

Tithi is a Hindu calendar (panchang), festival, muhurat and vrat guide app. We built it so that it works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Tithi does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your chosen city, month system, saved reminder dates (such as birthdays or anniversaries), followed vrats and ticked puja steps are stored only on your device. They never leave it. Deleting the app removes them.

Location

Tithi does not use GPS. You pick a city, or enter a latitude, longitude and time zone yourself; the calculations run on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the iPhone app schedules local reminders on your device for the dates you saved. No server is involved.

Children

Tithi does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
